    Why Mercury Powered Boats Get the Full Stack

    Tier 2 · SmartCraft Direct

    The data is already on the boat — we just route it.

    Every Mercury Racing 400R, 500R, and 600 SCI ships with SmartCraft — Mercury’s engine data protocol — bridging cleanly onto the NMEA 2000 backbone Howard already runs. A small cellular gateway aggregates the bus, buffers when out of signal, and pushes to the dashboard.

    For the customer that means richer data than the helm shows. For Howard’s service team it means fault codes and hour-meters land in the office before the boat’s back on the trailer.

    • ✓ Per-engine RPM, coolant, oil pressure, fuel flow at 1 Hz
    • ✓ Active and stored ECU fault codes (P-codes)
    • ✓ Throttle position, trim, alarms, and Smart Tow state
    • ✓ Fuel-burn integration over time & range estimates
    • ✓ Predictive service: 100-hr / 300-hr alerts before the chime
    • ✓ Same stack ships day-one on the next 288 with twin 500Rs
