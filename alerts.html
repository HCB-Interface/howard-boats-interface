How this works. Your Howard module logs ~40 metrics every run and at rest. We compare each metric to your boat's own day-one delivery baseline — not a generic spec sheet — and surface trends before they cross alarm thresholds. Predictions are based on trajectory slope, not single readings, so a one-off hot spike won't nag you and a slow drift won't slip by. When something looks worth a wrench, you'll see it here with a path straight to Howard Service. Nothing here gets shared with Howard unless you book service or grant access.
