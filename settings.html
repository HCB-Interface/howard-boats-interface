Sharing & ownership

By default, only you see this boat's location and live data. Use these controls to share temporarily, mark the boat as stolen, or transfer ownership at sale.

Stolen / panic mode

If the boat is stolen, switching to panic mode raises the heartbeat to every 5 minutes, sends maximum-priority alerts on every geofence breach, and generates a tracking link you can share with law enforcement.

Active location shares

No active shares. Anyone you share with will see your boat's live location for the duration you set, and nothing else.

Transfer ownership

When you sell the boat, transferring ownership migrates service history, hours, fault history, and gateway configuration to the new owner. Your account is unlinked from the hull when they accept.
